Research

F1 teams win on telemetry: decide what to measure, then trust the data. I ran my own quant study the same way. When three or more company insiders buy their own stock within 30 days, does the price follow?

Pre-registered. The signal, filters and tests were fixed before I saw a single result, so the finding couldn't be shaped afterwards.

The model proposes. Deterministic code decides.

Signature move: making evals stick. At Benefi nobody owned whether an LLM change was safe to ship; the check was eyeballing a few outputs. I built an eval and ran it in shadow mode. It caught a prompt change that looked fine overall but had broken one category. With caching and a fallback model it cost less than manual review, and it became the release gate for every change.
